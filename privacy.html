Privacy Policy & Data Protection Protocol

At CityHaldwani.com, protecting visitor confidentiality and advertiser anonymity is deeply embedded into our technical architecture. This document explains our data practices and zero-tracking philosophy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect names, residential addresses, financial payment credentials, or personal device identifiers from general website visitors. Browsing our classified listings is entirely anonymous.

2. Encrypted Third-Party Communications

When you click on Call or WhatsApp buttons, you transition directly to native phone dialers or end-to-end encrypted WhatsApp channels. CityHaldwani.com does not record, intercept, or inspect conversations between clients and independent companions.

3. Minimal Analytics Tracking

To evaluate marketing campaign efficacy and ensure functional contact routing, our serverless endpoints record aggregate metrics (e.g. total clicks on Call vs. WhatsApp buttons and general page URLs). No personal identifying information (PII) is attached to these event counts.

4. Anti-Scraping & Content Security

To shield independent advertisers from unauthorized image re-publication, our client-side scripts restrict right-click downloading, clipboard copying, and screenshot captures. Advertisers retain ownership rights to their proprietary promotional materials.
