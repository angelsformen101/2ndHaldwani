Report Violations & Policy Abuse

CityHaldwani.com enforces stringent advertising ethics. If you encounter any listing that violates our directory standards, contains stolen or uncredited photography, requests fraudulent upfront deposits, or promotes non-consensual behavior, please report it immediately.

Grounds for Immediate Listing Removal

  • Identity Misrepresentation: Use of celebrity, non-consenting individual, or counterfeit photos.
  • Advance Fee Fraud: Any advertiser demanding UPI or bank wire transfers before meeting in person.
  • Underage Content: Any indication or suspicion of individuals under 18 years of age.
  • Harassment or Blackmail: Any threatening behavior towards clients or directory staff.

How to File an Urgent Complaint

To file a violation ticket, message our administrative team on WhatsApp or call our central compliance desk with:

  1. The specific profile name and URL path where the listing is hosted.
  2. Screen captures or documentation supporting the complaint.
  3. A brief explanation of the infraction.
