Companion Safety Guidelines & Etiquette Protocol

Ensuring mutual safety, security, and respectful communication is the highest priority at CityHaldwani.com. Whether you are an experienced gentleman booking companionship in Haldwani or a traveler spending holidays in Nainital or Corbett, adhering to these standard safety tenets guarantees a seamless, stress-free encounter.

1. The Zero Advance Payment Rule

We advise all clients never to remit advance financial transfers, gift card codes, or booking tokens to unverified parties online. Reputable independent companions accept direct compensation in person upon arrival at your verified accommodation.

2. Book Recognized Accommodation Venues

Always arrange incall or outcall appointments at reputable, guest-friendly 4-star and 5-star hotels, luxury lakeside resorts, or upscale private villas. Avoid obscure, secluded guest houses where guest privacy and safety cannot be verified.

3. Clear Communication of Expectations

Before confirming an appointment, clearly communicate your preferred booking duration (e.g. 1 hour dinner date, 3 hours private companionship, or full overnight stay), expectations, and compensation over WhatsApp. Establishing clear agreements in advance prevents misunderstandings.

4. Hygiene, Discretion & Mutual Courtesy

Personal hygiene and respectful demeanor are non-negotiable standards. Escorts on our platform reserve the full legal right to decline service immediately if a client displays abusive behavior, signs of severe intoxication, or disregards agreed-upon personal boundaries.

5. Protecting Advertiser & Client Anonymity

Never attempt to record audio, take unauthorized photographs, or demand private personal identifying details (home addresses, college records, family background). Discretion is a two-way street that safeguards everyone involved.
